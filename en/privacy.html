Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · PassKeeper app (Android, Windows)

This policy describes how the PassKeeper app (the "App") processes user data. In one sentence: all your data stays with you — the App has no servers of its own and never collects or transmits your passwords or any other content to us.

1. Operator

The operator of the App is the owner of the pkeeper.ru domain (see "Contacts" for the support address). The operator does not receive or store user data on its own resources.

2. Data processed by the App

  • Safe contents (password cards, logins, notes, mnemonics) — stored only on the user's device as an encrypted database (AES; the encryption key is derived from the user's master password using PBKDF2). The master password is never stored or transmitted; the data cannot be recovered without it — including by the operator.
  • Registry of connected safes and settings — local app settings kept on the device.
  • Mnemonic phrases — generated and processed locally.

3. Sync via Google Drive

The cloud safe feature works only upon an explicit user action (connecting a cloud safe). In that case the App:

  • gets access to the user's Google Drive limited to storing safe files in the PassKeeper folder of the user's own Drive;
  • transfers encrypted safe files between the user's device and the user's own Google Drive — never to the operator's resources;
  • uses Google data for no purpose other than providing the sync feature, and complies with Google's API Services User Data Policy (limited use, no advertising transfer).
Sync can be turned off at any time: switch the safe to local (from the safe card menu) or delete the cloud safe — the Drive file is also deleted at the user's choice.

4. Android permissions

  • Notifications — for the quick-copy notification of a login/password (posted only when the user opens a card);
  • Biometrics — the device's system scanner is used; biometric data is processed by Android and never handed to the App;
  • Clipboard — data copied from the App is automatically wiped on a configurable timer.

5. What the App never does

  • no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no telemetry;
  • no access to contacts, SMS, geolocation, or files outside the safe files chosen by the user;
  • no selling or transferring data to third parties.

6. Data transfer to third parties

The only external system involved in processing is Google Drive (by explicit user action, section 3). Installation of the App may go through the RuStore store — data handling during installation follows the store's own rules.

7. Children's data

The App does not collect data from users of any age, including children. Age rating: Everyone (0+).

8. Storage and deletion

Users control all data themselves: deleting a safe or a card in the App removes the data irreversibly; cloud files are removed from the user's Google Drive by choosing the corresponding menu option. The operator keeps no copies.

9. Changes to this policy

The current version is always available on this page; significant changes update the date at the top.
