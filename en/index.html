If Google Play Protect shows a warning during installation

This is Android's standard screen for apps installed outside Google Play. Tap «Проверить» (Verify) — the scan completes with no findings. PassKeeper does not collect or send your data anywhere (see the privacy policy).

If Windows SmartScreen warns on first launch

The distribution is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen flags lesser-known programs. Click «Подробнее» (More info) → «Выполнить в любом случае» (Run anyway). PassKeeper does not collect or send your data anywhere (see the privacy policy).

If macOS warns on first launch

The distribution is not notarized by Apple yet, so Gatekeeper is stricter with apps outside the App Store. Install: open the DMG and drag PassKeeper to Applications. On first launch: right-click PassKeeper → «Открыть» (Open) → «Открыть» (Open) again in the dialog; or System Settings → Privacy & Security → «Открыть в любом случае» (Open Anyway). PassKeeper does not collect or send your data anywhere (see the privacy policy).

Features

i
The safe is an encrypted database: AES-256-GCM, key derived from the master password (PBKDF2, 200,000 iterations). Every field — login, password, notes, mnemonic — is encrypted separately with a random IV and an authentication tag. The master password is stored nowhere: even the developer cannot recover your data without it.

🔒 On-device encryption

A safe is an encrypted database (AES, key derived from your master password with PBKDF2). Nobody but you can decrypt the contents.

i
The master password is stored in the system secure storage (Android Keystore / Windows DPAPI), separately for each safe, and is unlocked only by a successful fingerprint scan. Biometrics are verified by the operating system itself — the app never sees your fingerprint. If the scan succeeds but the unlock fails (password changed on another device), the outdated key is wiped automatically.

🔐 Biometric unlock

Unlock your safe with a fingerprint — fast, no typing.

i
A cloud safe travels only between your devices and your own cloud — there are no developer servers. The provider sees nothing but the encrypted file. Card-level merge combines changes from two devices without losses. Multi-account: each safe is bound to its own cloud account, with sign-in free of re-logins and an «another account» guard.

☁️ Sync via your own Google Drive or Yandex Disk

A cloud safe lives in your Google Drive or Yandex Disk folder: data travels between your devices without touching the developer's servers.*

i
When you open a card, a shade notification appears with «Copy login» and «Copy password» buttons — one tap, no need to enter the app. The clipboard is cleared after a configurable timer; on Android 13+ the password is masked in the clipboard UI.

⚡ Quick copy

A shade notification copies your login or password with one tap, with automatic clipboard cleanup.

i
The generator builds a password from a coherent Russian phrase (dictionaries, ~10,000 words): subject, verb, object. First letters of the words, CamelCase and digits — easy to recall from the phrase, hard to brute-force. Configurable: number of words (2–5), letter slicing, capitals, leading digits.

🧠 Mnemonic passwords

The generator invents a coherent phrase and turns it into a strong password — easy to remember, hard to crack.

i
Folders with nesting and emoji icons, drag-and-drop card moving. Trash: deleted items are restored to their original folder. Import from browser password managers (Chrome, Firefox, etc.) via CSV.

🗂 Folders, trash, CSV

Folders with emoji icons, trash with restore, import from browser password CSVs.

i
A QR code or a .pkshare file, encrypted with a transfer password (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2): pass a card to another device directly, without any cloud. The transfer password is entered on both devices.

🔄 Card transfer

QR code or a .pkshare file — move a password card to another device without the cloud.

i
The safe locks: on a timer (1–5 minutes), when the app is backgrounded, and when the screen turns off (Android). Unlocking requires the password or biometrics again — your data is never left unattended.

⏱ Auto-lock

Locks on a timer, when backgrounded, and when the screen turns off — your data is never left unattended.

* We strongly advise against keeping your safes in multiple cloud accounts: PassKeeper is designed to work with one account per cloud (Google Drive and Yandex Disk), and switching between accounts slows down sign-in and can confuse synchronization.
